Language packs · USB installation

Your player.
Your language.

Build a translation pack or check one you already have. Your text and files stay in your browser.

01 / Make a pack

Start with your words.

Download the English template, translate the text after each =, then import it below. Any entry you leave out or empty uses English.

1–5 letters, numbers or hyphens: es, de, pt-BR.

The name shown in the player’s Language menu.

Keep the STR_ names and placeholders such as {0}. Write \n for a line break.

Paste or edit entries here. The native-name field and STR_LANGUAGE_NAME stay in step. Packs must fit in 18 KiB and use characters the player can display.

Ready to copy.

Download your pack, then follow the USB steps below.

02 / Check a pack

Have a pack already?

Check that it matches the current template, fits the player and has supported text before you copy it.

Load it into the editor to make changes and download a new pack.

Keep it readable.

The player currently supports English and Western European characters throughout its interface. This tool points out text it cannot display.

Use the template supplied with your firmware. If a firmware update changes the template, rebuild your pack from your saved translation text.

03 / Put it on your player

Copy. Eject. Choose.

  1. Connect to the computer. Plug in the ECHO and choose Connect to computer (or Home → USB mode → Connect to computer).
  2. Open your music drive. Use the microSD drive if a readable card is inserted; otherwise use internal storage. Settings → About → Music identifies the active storage.
  3. Copy the pack. Open or create .gnarlyr/lang on that drive and copy your downloaded file there. Keep its name: for example, es.pack. Keep a copy of any pack you replace and save your translation text on your computer.
  4. Safely eject every ECHO drive. Wait for the USB mode chooser, then select Player. There is no need to unplug or restart.
  5. Choose your language. Open Settings → Language and select its native name. The interface changes straight away; your choice is kept for the next start.

Keep the built-in English fallback available. To return to English, choose English in the Language menu. If an external pack is missing or incompatible, the player uses its built-in language.

A browser pack check uses this page’s template. If the player refuses the file, use the template matching your installed firmware and rebuild it.
