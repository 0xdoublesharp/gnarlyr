Install from FiiO firmware

  1. Verify the download. Compare the SHA-256 of ECHOECHO.img with its entry in SHA256SUMS.
  2. Copy. Connect the player and put ECHOECHO.img at the top of its internal drive, outside any folders.
  3. Eject and restart. Eject every player drive, unplug the cable, and fully turn the ECHO off and on with Power. Leave it powered while it installs.
  4. Check the version. After Gnarlyr starts, open Settings → About. Wait for the first library scan to finish before reconnecting USB.

Add your music

  1. Choose storage. Use a FAT32 or exFAT microSD card, internal storage, or both together. With a card inserted, the library includes both drives. Use Settings → Library → Eject card before removing a mounted card. Settings → About → Music shows Card + internal or Internal. Standard internal storage is named Gnarlyr; a card formatted on the player is named GnarlySD.
  2. Connect and copy. Plug into the computer and choose Connect to computer, or open Home → USB mode → Connect to computer. Copy music to the active drive in any folder layout.
  3. Eject and listen. Eject the player's drives. Let the library scan finish, choose Charge & play in the USB chooser, then open Music. Album covers fill in later while the player is idle.

Choose your Theme, Text size, and Controls in Settings. For a simple sound adjustment, open Equalizer → Sound shaping; for a precise headphone correction, use Parametric EQ. Explore the screens and buttons or add a language pack.

Update Gnarlyr

These steps apply to Gnarlyr 1.0 and later.

  1. Copy and check. In Connect to computer mode, put ECHOECHO.img outside any folders on internal storage, as its only update image. Check the copied file against the supplied checksum.
  2. Eject every drive. Eject internal storage and the microSD drive if present. Leave USB connected while the player installs.
  3. Wait, then restart. After “Update installed,” unplug USB, hold Power for two seconds to switch off, then turn it on again. Check the version in Settings → About.
  4. Confirm the new version. Reconnect in Connect to computer mode, copy a small file to internal storage, and eject every drive. Wait for “Saving this version as the backup” to finish before unplugging. The trial continues until a successful write and clean eject.

The updater removes the image after a successful install and keeps the previous firmware during the trial. If the new version starts without its text or exhausts its trial attempts, the player restores the backup and shows “Update rolled back.” Check Settings → About before trying another image.

Check the copied file on macOS

Check the image’s SHA-256

shasum -a 256 "/Volumes/YOUR_ECHO/ECHOECHO.img"

Replace YOUR_ECHO with the internal drive’s actual name, usually Gnarlyr, and compare the result with the image’s supplied checksum. On macOS, cp -X avoids extra metadata files; remove any ._ECHOECHO.img file beside the image.

Return to FiiO firmware

A working ECHO running Gnarlyr can return to stock with one file, ECHOV200.IMG.

  1. Download and unzip. Get the FiiO 2.0.0 firmware for the full-size ECHO.
  2. Copy to internal storage. In Connect to computer mode, copy the unchanged ECHOV200.IMG to the top level of the internal drive. Remove any earlier GNARLYR.IMG or ECHOECHO.IMG from that drive so the updater picks FiiO’s file.
  3. Eject and wait. Eject every player drive and keep USB connected until you see “FiiO firmware installed.” Unplug, remove the microSD card, then turn the ECHO off and on.

Installation troubleshooting

  • Update rolled back: the new version didn't start properly, so the player restored the previous version.
  • Update not recognized: check the filename, internal-storage location, and that it is the only update image.
  • Report a problem Open an issue ↗ with the version from Settings → About, what you did, and what happened. Connect the player once more, then attach the report files from the hidden .gnarlyr/debug folder on its internal drive.
  • Player hangs during a trial: hold Power for 10 seconds to restart it.
  • Player will not start: use the ECHO recovery guide. The guide covers the paperclip method and the Power and Vol+ shortcut, including which steps have been checked on the player.
  • Older custom firmware: follow the matching migration procedure before updating.
