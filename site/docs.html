Start here.

Use the volume buttons to move, Play to choose and Back to return. Hold Back to go Home.

Home

Hold Back → Home

Open Music, Audiobooks, Settings, Mixtapes, Listening or USB mode here. Now Playing and Up Next appear after you choose a track.

  • Press Play to open the highlighted item.
  • Press Power to turn the screen off or on; hold it for two seconds to switch off.
  • For pocket listening, hold Play and Power together for about a second to lock or unlock the buttons.

Find something to play.

Browse your library visually or search for a song. Start from an artist, album, folder or playlist to keep that group together.

Cover Flow

Home → Music → Cover Flow

Flip through album covers. The Random album tile picks an album for you.

  • Use the volume buttons to move between albums.
  • Hold to scroll faster, then jump between artist initials.
  • Press Play to open an album; hold Play to favorite it.

Search

Home → Music → Search

Find songs by title, artist, album, album artist, genre or file path.

  • Volume chooses a character; Play adds it and Menu erases.
  • Hold Play to run the search.
  • In the results, Menu opens sorting and filters. Hold Play on a song for Track info, My tags and queue actions.

Play now. Plan what comes next.

Now Playing controls the current track. Up Next shows its place in the play order.

Now Playing

Home → Now Playing

See the song, artist, album, audio format and progress. The top bar shows playback and connection status.

  • Play pauses or resumes. Hold Play to seek, choose a position with the volume buttons, then press Play to jump.
  • The volume buttons adjust loudness. Tap Menu to open Up Next; hold Menu for Lyrics.
  • Change playback shortcuts in Settings → Controls.

Up Next

Home → Up Next · or Menu on Now Playing

This is the order the player will follow, including songs you add while listening.

  • Hold Play on an entry for its actions.
  • Move a song earlier, later or next, or remove it.
  • On the playing entry, Edit full queue turns an album or shuffled order into a mix you can rearrange.

Save a mix. See what you listen to.

A mixtape saves a play order for later. Listening summarizes your time and the music you play most.

Mixtapes

Home → Mixtapes

Save queue captures Up Next as a named mix. New mixtape starts an empty one.

  • While naming, Volume chooses, Play adds and Menu erases; hold Play to save.
  • Open a saved mix to play it, edit it in Up Next, rename it or delete it.
  • Editing Up Next leaves the saved mix unchanged. In the mix’s actions, Replace queue saves your current play order over it.

Listening

Home → Listening

See total listening time, plays and skips, plus your top artists, albums and songs.

  • Open a top list to explore your most-played music.
  • Export plays writes an Audioscrobbler-compatible .scrobbler.log on the music drive.

Books, chapters and lyrics.

Audiobooks keep a bookmark so you can carry on later. Lyrics follow the words in a matching timed text file.

Audiobook chapters

Home → Audiobooks → hold Play on a book

M4B books appear on the Audiobooks shelf after a library scan, including books outside a dedicated folder.

  • Select a book to resume its saved chapter and position.
  • Hold Play to browse chapter titles, then choose one to start there.
  • Keep the book’s filename and location to retain its bookmark.

Audio formats and preparing speech recordings →

Lyrics

Now Playing → hold Menu

Put a timed .lrc file beside the song with the same name before the extension, such as Song.flac and Song.lrc.

  • The text follows playback.
  • Use the volume buttons to browse lines.
  • Back returns to Now Playing.

Try a different sound.

The equalizer changes the balance of bass, mids and treble. Listen to a preset, then keep it or return to your saved sound.

Equalizer

Settings → Equalizer

The graph shows the chosen EQ curve. It describes the filter’s response, not a live measurement of the music.

  • Use the volume buttons to preview presets; Play keeps one and Back cancels.
  • Custom EQ lets you choose a band, press Play to adjust it and press Play again to keep the change.
  • Save a curve to a slot to reuse it later. EQ applies to music up to 96 kHz; USB DAC audio uses its own path.

Make the screen and buttons yours.

Choose a look you can read comfortably, then assign shortcuts for the way you listen.

Themes

Settings → Theme

Choose Graphite, Neon Noir, Sunset, Sea Glass or Classic. Text size and Orientation control the layout.

  • Preview a theme with the volume buttons.
  • Play keeps it; Back restores your saved theme.
  • Language packs change interface text while keeping English available.

Controls

Settings → Controls

Assign tap and hold actions to Volume+, Volume−, Play and Menu. These shortcuts control playback; lists keep their usual navigation.

  • Choose a button action, then select the shortcut you want.
  • Use Default for the standard behavior.
  • Audio shortcuts also work with the screen off.

One cable. Three modes.

USB mode chooses who uses the player: you, your computer’s file manager or your computer’s audio output.

USB mode

Home → USB mode · or Settings → USB mode

  • Player: return to music while the cable stays connected.
  • USB storage: copy music or an update. Internal storage and an inserted microSD card appear separately.
  • USB DAC: send computer audio to the headphone outputs. Select Gnarlyr as the computer’s sound output.

Eject every ECHO drive before leaving Storage. Wait for the chooser, then select Player. From DAC mode, hold Back and release to return to the chooser.

For firmware updates, wait for “Update installed” before unplugging →
